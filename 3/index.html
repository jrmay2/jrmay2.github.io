Project Topic and App Description

Project Topic

My project will provide information regarding the aviation industry. I am interested in it due to my internship over the summer with the Jackson Municipal Airport Authority at Jackson Medgar Wiley Evers International Airport. My time there gave me insights into how airports operate and increased my overall interest in the industry. I believe that my project will help people who not only want to learn more about airports but also the types of airports in a specific region of the United States. Most importantly, this information will be presented in a digestible fashion so that any person will have no problem finding and understanding the information that they seek. The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) has a website that provides information about airports and their locations, but those topics are buried amongst a trove of other information that most people are not interested in or have any way to use effectively. This information, and other important points, will be boiled down to where they provide the most value possible with the least amount of effort on the part of the users. Equipped with their newfound information, users will be able to go more in-depth with other websites, such as the FAA or AirNav (AN), if they so desire.

App Description

Home Page

The home page will provide a brief overview of aiports and the aviation industry. This includes the basic information, like what an aiports is, and the different types of airports that exist. Some images will also be present. At minimum, there will three pictures: one of an airport, one of an airplane, and one of the staff that works at an airport, like air traffic controllers, baggage handlers, or pilots.

2-Step Page

First Step

The first choice of the page will ask the user to choose an airport size among these four choices:

  1. Nonhub Airport
  2. Small Hub Airport
  3. Medium Hub Airport
  4. Large Hub Airport

Second Step

The second choice of the page will provide users four airports of the previously chosen size to choose from. For example, say that the region of the website is the southern United States. If the user's first choice was "Small Hub Airport," then the second step will provide four small hub airports in the southern United States to choose from such as:

  1. Birmingham-Shuttlesworth International Airport (BHM)
  2. Little Rock National Airport / Bill and Hillary Clinton National Airport (LIT)
  3. Pensacola International Airport (PNS)
  4. Gulfport-Biloxi International Airport (GPT)

Read Page

The read page will have more in-depth information about the aviation industry and the roles that go into having airports operate smoothly. Things like airport operations, air traffic control, marketing, information technology, etc. will be provided here. In addition to the information, at least one image will accompany each topic as a visual aid.

Contact Page

The contact page will prompt users for their name and email address so that they can be addressed at a later time. There will also be a textbox for users to provide feedback about the website and its content or anything else they would like to share.
