Southwest Georgia Regional Airport (ABY)

Above: Southwest Georgia Regional Airport

Southwest Georgia Regional Airport (ABY) is a public airport located in Albany, Georgia, serving Albany and communities throughout southwestern Georgia. The airport provides regional passenger service and connects travelers to larger transportation hubs and destinations across the United States.

ABY features a passenger terminal with amenities such as rental car services, parking, and comfortable waiting areas. The airport plays an important role in connecting southwestern Georgia to the national air transportation system.
