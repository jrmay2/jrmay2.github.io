Greenville-Spartanburg International Airport (GSP)

Above: Greenville-Spartanburg International Airport

Greenville-Spartanburg International Airport (GSP) is a public airport located in Greer, South Carolina, serving the Greenville-Spartanburg area. It is the largest airport in the Upstate region of South Carolina and offers a range of domestic flights to major cities across the United States.

GSP has a single terminal with multiple gates and amenities for travelers, including dining options, retail shops, and car rental services. The airport is known for its efficient operations and friendly staff, making it a convenient choice for travelers in the region.
