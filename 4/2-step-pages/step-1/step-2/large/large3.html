Tampa International Airport (TPA)

Above: Tampa International Airport

Tampa International Airport (TPA) is a public airport located in Tampa, Florida, serving the Tampa Bay region and surrounding communities. The airport offers domestic and international flights to numerous destinations and is an important transportation hub for western Florida.

TPA is known for its convenient terminal design and features restaurants, retail shops, rental car services, parking, and other passenger amenities. The airport serves millions of travelers visiting Tampa and the surrounding Gulf Coast region.
